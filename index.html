The Science Behind It
Rainfall (mm) × Roof Area (m²) × Runoff Coefficient = Monthly Inflow in Litres
Simulated across all 12 months → Total Annual Harvest → ₹ Savings at ₹8 / 1000 L

The runoff coefficient accounts for your roof material — metal roofs lose only 10% to evaporation; terracotta loses up to 25%. We simulate month-by-month so overflow and dry spells are tracked accurately.

About
AquaHarvest.

How the simulation works, what the numbers mean, and what to keep in mind.

⚡ How it calculates
We apply the standard civil-engineering rainwater harvesting formula:
(Rainfall mm × Roof Area m² × Runoff Coeff.) = Monthly Inflow (L)
Inflow fills tank → overflow discarded → balance carries to next month
Annual Harvest = 12-month cumulative water used from tank
The simulation runs month-by-month, so overflow and dry spells are tracked precisely — not approximated.
🏠 Runoff coefficients explained
  • Metal Sheet — 0.90 · Smoothest surface, least evaporation
  • Concrete — 0.80 · Most common Indian rooftop
  • Terracotta — 0.75 · Most porous, lowest efficiency
💰 Cost savings methodology
Annual Harvest (L) ÷ 1000 × ₹8 = Annual Savings
₹8 / 1000 L is the national average municipal tariff. Your city may charge more — real savings could be higher.
📐 How the tank recommendation works
We silently test 10 standard sizes (500 L to 50,000 L) against your city and roof, scoring each by overflow volume and dry months. The size with the lowest penalty score is recommended.
⚠️ Limitations
  • Rainfall figures are historical monthly averages — actual years vary
  • First-flush contamination loss is not modelled — use a first-flush diverter
  • Evaporation, leakage and sediment losses inside the tank are not accounted for
  • Tariff is fixed at ₹8 — check your local municipal rate for accuracy
