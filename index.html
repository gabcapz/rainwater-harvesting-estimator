ℹ️ About This Estimator
How the simulation works, what the numbers mean, and its limitations.
⚡ How It Calculates
We use the standard rainwater harvesting formula that engineers and urban planners use:
(Rainfall mm × Roof Area m² × Runoff Coefficient) = Monthly Inflow (Litres)
Inflow fills the tank → overflow if full → emptied each month
Annual Harvest = Sum of all 12 months of water used from tank
Monthly rainfall data for each city is sourced from historical averages. The simulation runs month-by-month, carrying over leftover water from one month to the next — just like a real tank.
🏠 What the Runoff Coefficient Means
Not all rain that falls on your roof reaches the tank — some evaporates, some is absorbed. The runoff coefficient represents how efficiently water is collected:
  • Metal / GI Sheet — 0.90   Most efficient, very smooth surface
  • Asphalt Shingles — 0.85   Good efficiency, slightly porous
  • Concrete — 0.80   Common Indian rooftop, moderately efficient
  • Terracotta / Clay Tile — 0.75   Most absorption, least efficient
💰 How Cost Savings Are Calculated
We use ₹8 per 1,000 litres — the average Indian domestic water tariff across municipal corporations. Your actual rate may differ by city and slab.
Annual Harvest (L) ÷ 1000 × ₹8 = Annual Savings (₹)
This is a conservative estimate. If your municipality charges more (e.g. Delhi's ₹5–₹25 slabs), your real savings will be higher.
📐 How the Recommendation Works
After running your simulation, we also silently test 10 standard tank sizes (500 L → 50,000 L) for your city and roof. We then pick the size that gives the best balance of:
  • Minimising overflow (water you collect but cannot store)
  • Minimising dry months (months where the tank runs empty)
The optimal tank is the one with the lowest combined penalty score — real guidance, not a generic suggestion.
✅ Benefits
  • 🔍 See which months give you the most rainwater — plan usage accordingly
  • 💸 Get a realistic ₹ savings estimate tailored to your roof and city
  • 🌱 Understand your contribution to reducing municipal water demand
  • 📊 Visual 12-month chart makes overflow and dry spells obvious at a glance
  • 🔒 100% private — no signup, no server, all calculation happens in your browser
⚠️ Limitations
  • Rainfall data is based on historical monthly averages — actual year-to-year variation can be significant
  • Water tariff is fixed at ₹8/1000 L — your local rate may be higher or lower
  • First-flush loss (the initial dirty runoff) is not deducted — install a first-flush diverter in practice
  • Tank losses due to evaporation, leakage, or sediment are not modelled
  • Demand is not modelled — the simulation focuses purely on supply (inflow vs. tank capacity)
